### Goal: Implement a driveable arcade vehicle in Unreal Engine

I switched from the bee engine to Unreal Engine for three reasons. I am a lot more familiar Unreal, so I can spend more
time on actual features. Bee's tooling is also very limited, making it harder to iterate and debug quickly, and is
overall frustrating to work with. Finally, Unreal has significantly more resources and documentation available, which is
important when implementing features I haven't built before.

By the end of week 7, I will deliver a car in Unreal Engine that can accelerate, brake, reverse, and steer responsively
on a flat blocked-out track. The car will use Unreal's built-in physics & forces for suspension and collision, with
handling tuned for arcade feel. Success is measurable by the following:

Week 1-2: Car is on the ground with working suspension
Week 3-4: Acceleration, braking, and reversing all functional
Week 5-6: Steering implemented, car fully driveable and tuned
Week 7: Hand-in deliverables, learning log up-to-date
Week 8: Stretch goal — gear shifting integrated and driving feel further polished
Week 9-10: Prepare oral defense. Remaining time used for driving tuning and stretch goal progress.

Stretch goal: Gear shifting integrated so the car shifts through at least 3 forward gears automatically.
